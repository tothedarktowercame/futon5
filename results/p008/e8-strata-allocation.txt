# End(8) strata enumeration and sqrt-allocation (P-008b §4, §7 item 6)

SHEET reference, discovery only. Convention: all vectors in the
futon5 index order (Wolfram order); index(f) = sum_k f(k)*8^(7-k).
Stratum = (rank, cycle-part of f restricted to its recurrent set),
the §4 allocation key. Sizes are counts of maps, classes are
conjugation (functional-graph) classes inside the stratum.

Enumeration: one orbit-marking pass over 8^8 maps, 17.5 s.

Checks: sizes sum 16777216 (= 8^8 16777216: true); classes sum 951 (= 951: true); rank-8 strata 22 = 22, their sizes sum 40320 = 40320: true); classes by rank [1 14 75 221 314 229 75 22] (matches 7da77cc [1 14 75 221 314 229 75 22]: true); sizes by rank [8 7112 324576 2857680 7056000 5362560 1128960 40320] (matches section 2 Burnside [8 7112 324576 2857680 7056000 5362560 1128960 40320]: true).

Number of strata: 134.

Allocation rule (section 4 as written): n_s = max(2, round-half-up(alpha * sqrt(size_s))).
CONTRADICTION with section 4: the minimum 2 alone allocates 2 x 134 = 268 maps, which already EXCEEDS the
'aiming for about 150' target. No alpha makes the total 150; the smallest
alpha with total >= 150 is alpha ~ 0 (bisected to 1e-9: 0.000000001), at which every stratum sits AT the
minimum and the total is 268 (rounding is then irrelevant: the floor alternative gives the same 268).
For comparison, a non-degenerate sqrt allocation at target 300 uses
alpha = 0.004743959, total 300 (round-half-up).
