# Keyword-kind twin test — P-008b §7 item 6 (ruling 2026-11-15, exotype main 5176228)

SHEET reference. Predicate, exactly as written in the ruling:
`(= (sigma-of kind) (s8->sigma (kind->s8 kind)))`.

`s8->sigma` is private in grid.clj, so the evaluation script
(results/p008/e8-kind-twins-repl.clj) reaches it through
`#'futon5.exotype.grid/s8->sigma` and ALSO computes the public-path form
`(= (sigma-of kind) (sigma-of (kind->s8 kind)))`, which is the same
comparison: `sigma-of` on a keyword returns `(get propagators kind)`
(grid.clj:169-177) and on a permutation vector routes through `s8->sigma`
(grid.clj:171-173), so `sigma-of (kind->s8 kind)` IS `s8->sigma` of the
twin. Both columns agree on every kind (the "same" column).

The arms-discovery item-2 table (results/p008/e8-arms-plan.txt) used
`(= (sigma-of twin) (get propagators kind))` — the public-path half of
this predicate, i.e. the SAME comparison by the argument above. This
report adds the direct private-var form and the has-sigma column.

:even44 and :odd332 are NOT in `exotype-kinds` and no registered
P-008b arm uses them (checked in e8-arms-plan.txt item 2: :e8-stratified
draws End(8) vectors, :e8-bug adds the bug's 4 conjugate vectors,
:e8-v1 starts from S8 vectors or the 12 kinds). They are included
below for completeness, marked *.

Immutable kinds (predicate false, or no σ): **0 of the 12** (and 0 of the
14). Every kind has a σ and every twin is the same map as its kind.

Branch of the ruling that applies: **the "kinds stay" branch** — at most
2 immutable (here 0), so on a mutation hit a keyword kind is replaced by
`kind->s8 kind` and that vector is mutated (option (b)); `:e8-v1` keeps
"initial S8 or 12 kinds" — option (c), the S8-vector-start-only fallback,
is NOT triggered.

Test: test/futon5/exotype/e8_kind_twins_test.clj asserts the table (one
assertion per kind, ruling predicate via the public-path form and the
private-var form) plus a bad case: a deliberately wrong twin vector for
:collapser fails the predicate.
