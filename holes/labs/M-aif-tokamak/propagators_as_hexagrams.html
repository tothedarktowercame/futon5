Propagators as hexagrams

M-propagators, 2026-07-15. Every number here is measured; the correspondence is derived, not chosen.

The eight neighbourhoods ARE the eight trigrams. A cellular-automaton neighbourhood lcr is a three-line figure. That is not an analogy — it is the same object. So a propagator, which maps neighbourhood→neighbourhood, maps trigram→trigram, and each of its eight arrows is a hexagram: lower trigram = where you are, upper = where you are sent. The 64 hexagrams are exactly the 64 possible arrows.

The mapping checks itself

Nothing below was hand-assigned. The self-arrows — each trigram sent to itself — come out as exactly the eight doubled hexagrams, the only eight of the 64 that carry their trigram's own name. If the neighbourhood→trigram correspondence were off by any convention, this would not happen.

Both organising sets are here, at different levels

The 6-bit I Ching and the 8-bit iiching are not competing candidates. They are the hom-set and the subobject lattice of one small category.

In categorical terms: 8 objects, exactly one arrow per ordered pair — the codiscrete category on the trigrams. Its hom-set has 64 elements; its automorphism group is S8; its subobjects are the 256 rules. A sigil is a predicate on the trigrams, whether or not anything reads it.

The theorem, and what it forbids

Proved (exhaustive over 256 bytes × 8 actions, commit dbb8453): the live twin rotate+2 and the dead twin (0 1 2 3)(4 5 6 7) are exactly conjugate via τ = [0 4 1 5 2 6 3 7]. Relabelling by τ maps every trajectory of one onto the other. So every intrinsic property of σ alone — cycle type, orbit count, orbit length, attractor size, freeze time, gcd — is identical for a σ that lives and one that dies. No σ-only property can work.

But that theorem also says where to look. A working property must not be τ-invariant. So: what does τ preserve?

τ preserves the left bit, always. It fixes the two poles — 000 (坤 Kun, earth) and 111 (乾 Qian, heaven) — and 3-cycles the (centre,right) pairs within each half: (001 011 010)(100 101 110).

Consequence: any property about the poles alone, or about left-neighbour structure alone, is τ-invariant and provably cannot discriminate. "Are heaven and earth in the same orbit?" is dead on arrival. The discriminator, if it exists, must distinguish the six middle trigrams in a way τ scrambles.
What is NOT claimed. The structure above is exact and checkable. The meanings — that the living propagator reads as Holding Together / Inner Truth / Fellowship / Gathering while Figure 8's revolution ends in 泰 Peace and dies — are the Yijing's traditional interpretations. They are suggestive and they are not evidence. Reading them as prediction is numerology until one of them predicts something we did not already know. A test is available: the index is computing all 20,256 orbits. If live/dead correlates with any hexagram-level property of σ, that would be a σ-only property — which the theorem forbids. So the honest prediction is that it will not correlate, and if it does, something is wrong with the theorem.
