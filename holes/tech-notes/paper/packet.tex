\vspace{1em}
\noindent This packet contains the paper and its four supplements as a single
file. The paper is self-contained: it states each definition, theorem and
empirical finding in prose where it is used, and the supplements carry the
formal statements and proofs, the findings notebook, the additional figures, and
one exploratory analysis that is reported but not relied upon. Supplement~4
records a measure that was withdrawn after its mask and measure proved circular;
it is included for completeness and is not a premise of the argument.
