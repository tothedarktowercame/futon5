\vspace{1em}
\noindent This packet contains the paper and its four supplements in one file.
The paper is self-contained: it states each definition, theorem and empirical
finding in prose where it is used, and the supplements carry the formal
statements and proofs, the findings notebook, the additional figures, and one
exploratory analysis that is reported but not relied upon. Supplement~4 records
a measure withdrawn after its mask and measure proved circular; it is included
for completeness and is not a premise of the argument.

% --- supplements -----------------------------------------------------
% Each begins on a fresh page under its own heading. Float counters reset so
% the supplements number as they do standalone; their \thefigure carries the
% supplement number (S3.1, S4.1), so no two floats share a printed number.
% Supplements sit at \part level, as peers of Parts I and II. That is what makes
% their own \section headings nest beneath them in the contents and the PDF
% outline instead of appearing as siblings.
%
% Two forms, because the sources differ: supplements 1 and 4 are full apa7
% documents that call \maketitle and carry no heading of their own, while 2 and
% 3 open with their own \section*. Emitting a heading for the latter would print
% it twice, so they get the contents line only.
% Float counters restart per supplement and their printed numbers carry the
% supplement number (S3.1, S4.2), so nothing collides with the paper's
% Figures 1--16. The sources set this in their own preambles for standalone
% builds; docmute discards those, so it must be set again here.
\newcommand{\packetsuppstart}[1]{%
  \clearpage
  \setcounter{figure}{0}\setcounter{table}{0}%
  \renewcommand{\thefigure}{S#1.\arabic{figure}}%
  \renewcommand{\thetable}{S#1.\arabic{table}}%
  \phantomsection}
\newcommand{\packetsupp}[2]{%
  \packetsuppstart{#1}%
  \part*{Supplement #1: #2}%
  \addcontentsline{toc}{part}{Supplement #1: #2}}
\newcommand{\packetsuppself}[2]{%
  \packetsuppstart{#1}%
  \addcontentsline{toc}{part}{Supplement #1: #2}}
