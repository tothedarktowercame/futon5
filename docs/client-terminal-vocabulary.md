# Client Terminal Vocabulary (Draft)

The dual of `sales-terminal-vocabulary.md`.  Where
`cyberants-terminal-vocabulary.md` inventories the wiring points of a
*virtual* ant you mint and program, this draft models a **client as a
co-evolving organism** — one you do **not** mint, program, or wire.

Two asymmetries vs. cyberants:

1. **Minting.** Cyberants are spawned for free; clients are scarce and
   acquired only through the sales cycle (see the dual doc).  "Make a runner"
   here is a *cost*, not an instantiation.
2. **Firing rights.** In the ant model the operator chooses the ant's
   actions.  Here, **the client's action terminals are consent-gated and
   operator-unfireable** — only the client fires them.  This is WM-I4 / the
   consent gate applied to people: you may *invite* a decision, never *make*
   it.  Willingness-to-buy is the client pressing their own gate.

The client and the **sales organism** are coupled, not controller↔controlled:
the sales organism's *action terminals* are this organism's *observations*,
and vice versa.  Healthy coupling is **mutualism** (both fitnesses rise);
parasitism (extraction without client benefit) is the failure mode the dual
doc forbids.

## Client Observations (what the client senses)

Largely the sales organism's actions, plus the client's own context:

- :offer — a proposition put to the client (scope, price).
- :demonstration — evidence the thing works (a demo, a delivered fix).
- :prior-delivery — track record with this client (e.g. Invoices #1–#3).
- :social-proof — others' uptake / reputation signals.
- :fit-signal — perceived match to the client's own goals (see constraints).
- :budget-pressure / :deadline — the client's own external forcing.
- :follow-up — being kept warm vs. going cold.

## Client Internal State (latent; NOT operator/sales-writable)

- :interest-match — overlap between the offer and the client's interest
  territories (the mutualistic "sigil"; Interest Network is the prior art).
- :trust — confidence built from prior delivery + register.
- :urgency — the client's own time-pressure.
- :budget — capacity and willingness to pay.
- :satisfaction — value actually received so far.

These are *inferred*, never set.  The sales organism updates a *belief* over
them (a posterior), it does not write them.

## Client Action Terminals (consent-gated — only the client fires)

- :engage — opens a conversation / takes a meeting.
- :agree-to-proceed — verbal/written go-ahead on a scope.
- :pay — issues / settles an invoice (the canonical fire).
- :renew — continues into a next cycle.
- :refer — introduces another prospective client (the runner reproduces).
- :disengage — goes cold / declines (legitimate; not a failure to "fix").

The operator/sales organism may *observe* and *invite* these; it may never
fire them.  `:pay` is the client's own consent gate.

## Interest-match constraints (the offer fires only where it fits)

An offer should be put only where a genuine **interest-territory overlap**
exists — the client benefits, not just Hyperreal.  No overlap → the right
move is `:disengage`, not harder pushing (that is the parasitic gradient).
Cf. the bipartite Interest Network (essays × interest-territories).

## Outcome / Utility (the client's)

- value received, goals advanced, problem closed, capability gained.
A "sale" that scores high for the seller and low here is **parasitic** and
fails the mutualism invariant — negative over any horizon longer than one
transaction.

## Coupling / duality

Client `:action-terminals` → sales `:observations` (willingness signals).
Sales `:action-terminals` → client `:observations` (offers/demos).
Coupling medium: interest-territory overlap.  See
`sales-terminal-vocabulary.md` for the dual organism and the mutualism
invariant.

## Worked example — Eric White / Oxford Brookes (VSAT POC)

An **already-minted, mutualistic client-ant**.  :interest-match high (VSAT
serves Brookes' community-sensemaking + pedagogy goals); :trust high
(Invoices #1–#3 delivered + paid); :prior-delivery strong.  `:agree-to-proceed`
fired verbally on the VSATLATARIUM continuation (2026-05-26).  This yields the
Future-stratum prior `p≈0.7, £4500` — a high firing-probability *because the
match and delivery are real*, not because anything was installed.  A cold
prospect is the same vocabulary with low :interest-match and an unpaid minting
cost (the sales organism's metabolic burden).
